Privacy policy

Pharmacology Made Visual · Last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Pharmacology Made Visual ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett, trading as Science Made Visual ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • You don't need an account. Your flashcard progress, quiz scores and settings stay on your device.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • Videos stream from our video host, which (like any website) sees your IP address to deliver them.
  • The app has no ads and no ad tracking.

We don't collect your name, email, health information or contacts through the app, and nothing you type in the app is sent to us.

Purchases

The app is sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Links to other sites

The app links to YouTube, TikTok, Etsy, our website and guidance from NICE, the BNF, the MHRA, the NHS and others. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

On-device data stays until you reset progress (Account → Reset progress) or delete the app. Video host logs are kept for up to 30 days.

Children

The app is designed for university students and healthcare professionals and is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because we hold almost nothing that identifies you, the quickest way to delete everything is to delete the app. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
