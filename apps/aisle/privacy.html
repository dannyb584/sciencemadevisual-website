Privacy policy

Aisle: Pencil Shopping List · Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Aisle: Pencil Shopping List ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you make in the app is saved on your device, and a list you share with your household goes through your own iCloud to the people you invite. We can't see it.
  • Aisle doesn't ask for your photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location. iOS may ask once whether Aisle can show your list on the Lock Screen while you shop, and sharing uses your own iCloud account, only when you share or join a list.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of ours.

None of this is sent to us. A list you choose to share goes through your own iCloud to the people you invite, and they can see and change it; everything else stays on your phone.

Purchases

The app costs £0.99 to download, once. Extras are sold as in-app purchases through Apple. Apple takes any payment and we never see your card details; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

How long data is kept

Swipe a list in Lists and shops to delete it, and use Forget my aisle corrections in Settings to clear what Aisle has learned. Deleting a list you share, or tapping Stop sharing, removes it from iCloud; the people you shared it with keep the copy on their own phones as an ordinary list. Leave this list does the same for a list someone shared with you. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on that device; stop sharing first if you also want a shared list gone from iCloud.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own devices, you're in control of all of it. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
