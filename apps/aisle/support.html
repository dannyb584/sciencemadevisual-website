Questions

Why doesn't my list sort into aisles on my iPhone?
The sorted aisles are on the iPhone Duo's open screen. On other iPhones, and on the Duo's cover, the list stays in the order you wrote it; your Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show it in walking order.
Something went in the wrong aisle.
Long-press it (or tap its dots on the open screen), choose Aisle and wording and pick the right aisle. On the open screen you can also drag it onto an aisle heading. Aisle remembers and puts it there next time. Settings can forget your corrections.
It couldn't read my writing.
It waits in Other, still in your handwriting. Long-press it, choose Aisle and wording, and type what it says or tap Read my writing again.
Can I write with my finger?
Yes. Fingers write until you first use Apple Pencil; after that the Pencil writes and fingers scroll. Tap the circle beside an item to tick it with a finger, or choose "Pencil and finger" in Settings to always write and tick with both.
Can I put the aisles in my shop's order?
Yes. Open Settings, tap Aisle order and drag the aisles into the order you walk round. With Aisle Household, each shop's list keeps its own order.
How do I share a list with my household?
Aisle Household (£2.99, one payment, not a subscription) adds sharing. Open Settings and tap Share under Household, then send the invitation by Messages, Mail or a link. Everyone needs to be signed in to iCloud. Joining a list someone shares with you needs no purchase.
What does the coffee in Settings do?
Buy Danny a coffee is a tip, if you'd like to say thanks. It doesn't unlock anything.
How do I stop sharing?
Open Settings and tap Stop sharing (or Leave this list, if someone shared it with you). The list stays on each phone; changes just stop going back and forth.
I bought Aisle Household on another phone.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchases.
Where are my lists kept?
On your phone. A list you share is also kept in the sharer's iCloud and on the phones of the people they invite. Deleting the app deletes what it kept on that phone.
