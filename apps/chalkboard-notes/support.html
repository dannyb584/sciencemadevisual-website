Questions

How do I wipe the whole board?
Tap the duster to pick it up, then tap it again: it sweeps the board clean. Undo puts everything back.
Can I draw with my finger?
Yes. Fingers draw until you use an Apple Pencil; after that the Pencil draws and fingers scroll. Choose "Pencil and finger" in Settings to always draw with both.
How do I get a broad, bold line?
Tap the chalk you're holding again to turn it on its side.
Can I make the chalk thinner or thicker?
Tap the dot beside the chalk for fine, medium or bold, or pick one in Settings.
I bought the Chalk Box on another phone.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases.
Where are my boards kept?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them, so share or save a picture of any board you want to keep.
