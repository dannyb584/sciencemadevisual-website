Privacy policy

Cover Notes: Pencil Notes · Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Cover Notes: Pencil Notes ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you make in the app is saved on your device. We can't see it.
  • The only permission the app asks for is Reminders, and only when you tap Send to Reminders (a Plus feature), after it explains why. iOS calls it full access because Reminders has no add-only option, but Cover Notes only adds the actions you send to its own list, and doesn't read or change your other reminders.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of ours.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your notes (the handwriting, what it was read as, your corrections, tags and actions)On your deviceSo your notes are there when you come back
A short summary of your latest noteOn your device, shared with the app's widgetsTo show it on your Home Screen and Lock Screen
Your notes' titles, text, tags and a small picture of eachOn your device, in iOS's own search indexSo you can find a note from Spotlight (you can turn this off in Settings)
Actions you send to Reminders (Cover Notes Plus)In your own Reminders, in a list called Cover NotesYou choose to send them; the app only adds and never reads your other reminders
Your settings (fresh page time, paper, Pencil, Spotlight, sounds)On your deviceSo the app works the way you set it
Notes you share or exportWherever you send themYou choose
Emails you send usOur mailboxAnswering you

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

The app is free to download. Extras are sold as in-app purchases through Apple. Apple takes any payment and we never see your card details; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

How long data is kept

Delete a note from your notes list, or press and hold it in the recent strip, to remove it and its place in Spotlight. Reminders you sent stay in Reminders until you delete them there. Deleting the app removes everything else it kept on that device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own devices, you're in control of all of it. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
