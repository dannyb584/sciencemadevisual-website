Questions

How do I start a new page?
Swipe left across the page (one finger if you write with Apple Pencil, two if your finger draws), or tap the new page button. Come back within a minute and your last note reopens; you can make that 5 or 15 minutes in Settings.
It read a word wrong.
Tap the line in the tidy view and type what it says. Your correction stays, even if you write more on that note. A line it can't read keeps its ink and says "Tap to type what it says".
How do I make an action?
Draw a circle round a line. It appears in the note's Actions box to tick off, and with Cover Notes Plus you can send it to Reminders.
Why does it ask for full access to Reminders?
Reminders has no add-only permission. Cover Notes only adds the actions you send to a list called Cover Notes; it doesn't read or change your other reminders.
Can I write with my finger?
Yes. Fingers draw until you use an Apple Pencil; after that the Pencil draws and fingers scroll. Choose "Pencil and finger" in Settings to always draw with both.
Which languages does it read?
English handwriting. Anything it can't read stays as your ink.
How do I cancel Plus?
In the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Cover Notes Plus. Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
I subscribed on another phone.
Open Settings in Cover Notes and tap Restore purchases.
Where are my notes kept?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them, so share or export any note you want to keep.
