Privacy policy

Scribble Alive · Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Scribble Alive ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you make in the app is saved on your device. We can't see it.
  • The only permission the app asks for is to add videos to Photos, and only when you tap Save video to Photos. It can't see your library. Guessing what you drew happens on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence, and your drawings never leave it.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of ours.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your creatures (each drawing, its name, what it became, its colours and move) and your Book of friendsOn your deviceSo your zoo is there when you come back
What Scribble has learned: the outline of each drawing you named (as 48 points, not the picture), the name, and any things you taught itOn your deviceSo it knows your way of drawing things next time
A small picture of your selected creatureOn your device, shared with the app's widgetTo show it on your Home Screen
Your settings (pen, colour and size, sounds, the grown-ups' check, the watermark) and your days in a rowOn your deviceSo the app works the way you left it
Videos, GIFs and stickers you makeWherever you send or save themYou choose; the app only keeps a temporary file while sharing
Emails you send usOur mailboxAnswering you

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

The app is free to download. Extras are sold as in-app purchases through Apple. Apple takes any payment and we never see your card details; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

How long data is kept

Clear empties a drawing on any iPhone. On iPhone Duo opened out, touch and hold a creature in the row at the top of the drawing page and choose Delete. Settings, then "Forget what it has learned", clears Scribble's memory. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on that device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own devices, you're in control of all of it. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
