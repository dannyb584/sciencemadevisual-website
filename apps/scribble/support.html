Questions

How does Scribble know what I drew?
It looks at your drawing with Apple Intelligence on your iPhone, matches what it sees to the 1,575 things it knows, and remembers drawings you've named before. Your drawing never leaves your phone.
It guessed wrong. How do I put it right?
Tap Not quite (or Transform, then Change) and pick the right thing from the list or the search. Scribble remembers, so the next drawing like it is recognised straight away.
My iPhone doesn't have Apple Intelligence.
Scribble still works. It knows its 24 cartoon friends by shape, remembers what you teach it, and when it isn't sure it lets you pick from the list. Settings says which way it's guessing on your phone.
Can I teach it something that isn't on the list?
Yes. Search for it in the Transform list, tap Teach Scribble, then choose how it moves and its colours.
Can it ask me before any purchase or share?
Turn on Kids mode in Settings. A grown-ups' question then comes up before any purchase, share or Settings.
How do I cancel Scribble Plus?
In the Settings app: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Plus stays on until the end of the period you've paid for.
I bought something on another phone.
Open Settings in Scribble and tap Restore purchases. It's on every purchase sheet too.
Where are my creatures kept?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them, so save a video of any you want to keep.
