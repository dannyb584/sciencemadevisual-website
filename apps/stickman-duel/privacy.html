Privacy policy

Stickman Duel · Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Stickman Duel ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you make in the app is saved on your device. We can't see it.
  • The app doesn't ask for any permissions: no Photos, camera, microphone, location or notifications.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of ours.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
The weapons you draw in each corner, and what each was taken to beOn your deviceSo a duel you've started is still there when you come back
Your drawing, while the phone works out what it isLooked at by Apple's model on your iPhone, then let goTo name the weapon you drew. It is never sent anywhere or kept
The score: wins for each corner, and the weapons that wonOn your device, shared with the app's widgetsTo keep score, and show it on your Home Screen and Lock Screen
Your settings (sounds, haptics, Pencil or finger)On your deviceSo the app works the way you set it
Emails you send usOur mailboxAnswering you

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

The app is free to download. Apple takes any payment and we never see your card details; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

How long data is kept

Clear rubs out a corner's drawing, and Settings has Reset the score. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on that device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own devices, you're in control of all of it. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
