Questions

How does it know what I drew?
Your iPhone's own Apple Intelligence model looks at the drawing, on the phone, and the game matches what it sees to its list of 80 weapons. When it's only guessing, the name has a question mark and Battle asks you first, with its guesses at the top of the list. Tap the name any time to change it.
My iPhone doesn't read my drawings.
Reading drawings needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence switched on (Settings, Apple Intelligence & Siri). Without it you can still play: draw for fun and pick your weapon from the list, or spin the wheel.
Can I play on my own?
Yes: draw both weapons, or spin both, and watch them fight. It's made for two, though, one corner each.
Why did my weapon lose?
Every fight is made up on the spot. Some weapons have the edge over others, but no exchange is ever a sure thing, so the underdog can win. The line after the fight says what decided it.
Are there ads or a subscription?
No. You pay £0.99 once and every weapon is included. The coffee tips in Settings are only a thank-you and unlock nothing.
Can I turn the sound off?
Yes. Settings has switches for Sounds and Haptics, and the game stays quiet when your iPhone is on silent.
How do I reset the score?
Tap the gear to open Settings, then Reset the score.
Where are my drawings kept?
Only on your phone. Nothing you draw is sent anywhere, not even to us.
