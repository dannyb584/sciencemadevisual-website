Privacy policy

Strike: Pencil To-Do · Last updated 25 November 2026

Who we are

Strike: Pencil To-Do ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • What you make in the app is saved on your device (and, if you turn on Reminders sync, tasks with a date are also added to a list called Strike in your Reminders app). We can't see it.
  • The app asks for one permission, and only if you turn on Reminders sync (Strike Plus): full access to Reminders, so it can add a list called Strike and see what you tick off there. It only changes that list. iOS may also ask once whether Strike can show its Live Activity on the Lock Screen.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of ours.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your pages and tasks (your handwriting, what it was read as, dates, names, tags and what's done)On your deviceSo your list is there when you come back

Tasks with a date, only if you turn on Reminders sync (Strike Plus)In a list called Strike in the Reminders app on your device, and your iCloud if your Reminders use itSo they show in Reminders, and ticking one off there strikes it off in Strike
Your settings (drawing input, tidy writing, sounds, Lock Screen) and your streakOn your deviceSo the app works the way you set it

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

The app is free to download. Extras are sold as in-app purchases through Apple. Apple takes any payment and we never see your card details; Apple's privacy policy applies to it.

How long data is kept

Scribble out a task to delete it, or delete a page from its menu. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on that device. Anything already in the Strike list in Reminders stays there until you delete it in Reminders.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own devices, you're in control of all of it. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
