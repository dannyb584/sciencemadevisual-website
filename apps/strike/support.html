Questions

How do I strike a task off?
Draw a line through it on the page. On the plan or the cover, a tap works too. To bring it back, rub the line out with the eraser, or hold it in Done and tap Not done.
What do the other pen gestures do?
Circle a task to put it in Today, draw an arrow to move it to Later, and scribble over it to delete it (Undo shows for a few seconds). The ? button on the page shows them all.
How do I give a task a date?
Write it in the task, like "Fri" or "tomorrow". Or hold the task on the plan and tap Set a date.
Strike read my writing wrong.
Hold the task on the plan and tap Fix the reading. Your handwriting stays as it is; the text you type is used for dates, names and tags.
Can I type a task instead?
Yes. Tap the keyboard button on the page or the cover, and the task is written onto the page for you.
How do I see today's tasks on the Lock Screen?
Open Strike with something in Today and allow it when iOS asks. While your list is under way it shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. You can turn it off in Settings, under At a glance.
How does Reminders sync work?
With Strike Plus, turn on Sync with Reminders in Settings and allow access. Tasks with a date go into a list called Strike in Reminders; tick one off there and it's struck off in Strike. Tasks without a date stay in Strike, and Strike only changes its own list.
How do I cancel Strike Plus?
In the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Strike. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.
I bought Strike Plus on another phone.
Open Settings in Strike and tap Restore purchases (under About), or tap Restore purchases on the Strike Plus screen.
Where are my tasks kept?
Only on your phone, and in Reminders if you turn on the sync. Deleting the app deletes your pages and tasks.
