Questions

How do I tick something?
Draw a tick in its box with Apple Pencil or a finger, or just tap the box. A cross means not needed; a scribble clears it. Touch and hold a box for Tick, Not needed and Clear.
What are the initials for?
Lists such as Opening up have a small box beside each item for initials. Write them there, or put your initials in Settings and they're added when you tick with a tap.
Can I write my own list?
Yes, as many as you like. Tap Write your own and write one thing per line; each line becomes an item. You can type items too.
My writing shows as neat text. Can I see my own?
Turn off Tidy my writing in Settings. Your handwriting is always kept.
How do I get my list on the Lock Screen?
Start a list and it shows on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while it's under way (allow it if iOS asks). You can turn it off in Settings, under At a glance. For a widget, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and search for Tick.
I bought a pack on another phone.
Open Settings and tap Restore purchases.
Where are my lists kept?
Only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them, so export a PDF of any list you want to keep.
