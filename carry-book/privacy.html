Privacy policy

Carry Book · Last updated 9 November 2026

Who we are

Carry Book ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your yardage book is saved on your device. If you use iCloud, it's also kept in your own private iCloud, so it's the same on your iPhone and iPad. We can't see it.
  • The app doesn't use your location and doesn't ask for any permissions.
  • The app doesn't connect to any server of ours. The only connection it makes is to Apple's iCloud, for you, if you use it.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone sends the book to your Apple Watch directly. Data in your private iCloud is held by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; only you can see it.

Purchases

The app is distributed by Apple through the App Store. Apple takes the payment and we never see your card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

How long data is kept

Everything stays until you change it. To clear your own numbers and notes, open Customise and choose Start again from the sample; to clear the bookplate, delete what's in it. Both changes reach your iCloud and your other devices. Deleting the app removes what's on that device.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own devices and your own iCloud, you're in control of all of it, as described above. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
