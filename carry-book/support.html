Support

Carry Book, by Danny Bartlett

Questions, problems or ideas? Email danny@pcce.co.uk and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Your book is saved on your device, and in your own iCloud if you use it.

How do I put in my own numbers? Turn to a club's page and tap the pencil (Edit). Use − and + to set the carry, total and loft, and tap the notes line to write a note to yourself. Tap Done when you've finished.

How do I choose the clubs in my bag? Tap the palette (Customise), then In the bag. You can carry 13 clubs plus your putter, the 14 the rules allow.

Can I use metres? Yes. Customise → Distances in → Metres.

Is my book the same on my iPad? Yes, if both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Changes can take a moment to arrive.

How do I get my book on my Apple Watch? Install Carry Book on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone, then open Carry Book on your iPhone with your watch nearby. Until then the watch shows the sample bag. Turn the Digital Crown to move between clubs.

How do I start again? Customise → Start again from the sample. Your binding, paper and bookplate stay as they are.

Does it use GPS? No. Carry Book holds your own club distances; it isn't a course guide and doesn't use your location.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
