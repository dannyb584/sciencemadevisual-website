Privacy policy

Essay Blueprint · Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

Essay Blueprint ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your essays, briefs, notes and sources are kept on your iPhone or iPad. They're never sent to us.
  • The app only goes online when you look up a source, and then it sends just the DOI, ISBN, link or reference text you gave it.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your essays: briefs, plans, word counts, notes and sourcesOn your device, inside the appSo you can plan and come back to them
Your settings (default referencing style, allowance, Apple Intelligence on or off)On your device, inside the appSo new essays start the way you like
Emails you send usOur mailboxAnswering you

Your essays aren't sent to us or to anyone else, and the app doesn't sync them to iCloud. They're included in your device's own backup, if you have backups turned on.

Looking up a source

When you ask the app to look up a source, it sends only what you gave it, straight from your device:

You giveSent to
A DOI, or the text of a referenceCrossref (api.crossref.org), the registry of scholarly publications
An ISBN, typed or scanned from a barcodeOpen Library (openlibrary.org), run by the Internet Archive
A link to a web pageThat web page, to read the details it publishes about itself

Nothing else goes with it: no name, account, device identifier or essay content. Like any website, these services can see your device's internet address while they answer. They aren't partners of ours and we receive nothing from them. Their own privacy policies apply.

Done on your device

Reading a photo of a brief or a reference, scanning a book's barcode and suggesting sections with Apple Intelligence all happen on your iPhone or iPad. The camera is only used when you choose to scan, and photos are only read when you pick one.

Purchases

The app is free to download, with a one-off in-app purchase to unlock every essay. Apple takes the payment and we never see your card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

How long data is kept

Your essays stay until you delete them (swipe left on an essay) or delete the app, which removes everything it keeps.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own device, you're in control of all of it, as described above. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
