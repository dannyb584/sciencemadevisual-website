Common questions

How do I start? Tap + on the Essays screen, paste your brief (or tap Photo or Dictate) and tap Make plan. The two sample essays show what a finished plan looks like.

Does it write my essay? No. Essay Blueprint plans the essay with you: the sections, the words for each and the sources you'll use. The writing is yours.

How are the word counts worked out? The app reads the word count from your brief (and Part A, Part B and so on, if it has them), gives the introduction and conclusion a fixed share, and shares the rest between the main sections. Change any number and the other sections in that part make room. Lock a section to keep its words while you change the others.

Where do the sections come from? On an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence turned on, the sections are suggested from your brief by Apple's model, on the device. Without it, you get a standard structure for the type of essay. You can rename, add, remove and move sections either way. To turn Apple Intelligence on, open the Settings app → Apple Intelligence & Siri.

Why didn't a look-up find my source? Look-ups need the internet. DOIs are looked up on Crossref, which covers most journal articles but not every DOI; ISBNs are looked up on Open Library, which doesn't have every book. If a look-up comes back empty, tap Type the details.

Are the references right for my course? They follow Harvard (Cite Them Right), APA 7th, Vancouver, MLA 9th, Chicago author-date and IEEE. Many courses have their own version of a style, so check against your course's guide, and edit a source's details if something's missing.

How do I get my plan into Word? On the Reading list tab, Copy for Word copies the list with its italics and links, and Word file shares it as a .docx. Essay outline shares your headings, word targets, notes and sources as a Word file.

Can I use it on my iPhone and iPad together? Not yet. Your essays stay on the device you made them on.

I've bought the unlock. How do I get it on a new device? Settings → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.

The samples have gone. Settings → Add the sample essays again.

Refunds. Purchases are handled by Apple: request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
