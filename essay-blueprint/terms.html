1. About the app

Essay Blueprint is an essay planner for iPhone and iPad, made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). It turns an assignment brief into a plan with word counts and keeps your sources and references. Your use of the app is also covered by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

2. Your work

Essay Blueprint helps you plan. It doesn't write essays and it doesn't choose sources for you. Your essay, and following your school, college or university's rules on academic integrity, are your responsibility.

3. Plans and references

Suggested sections and word counts are a starting point: check them against your brief and your tutor's guidance. References are formatted to the published style guides, but courses often have their own versions, so check them against your course's referencing guide before you hand in. Source details come from Crossref, Open Library or the page you linked, and can be incomplete or wrong; you can edit them.
