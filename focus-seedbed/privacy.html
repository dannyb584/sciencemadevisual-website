Privacy policy

Focus Seedbed · Last updated 6 November 2026

Who we are

Focus Seedbed ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your garden, settings and any session in progress are saved only on your device.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Plants you've finished growing, with the date and how long you focusedOnly on your deviceTo show your garden
Your chosen seed, focus time and any session in progressOnly on your deviceSo the timer carries on if you close the app
Emails you send usOur mailboxAnswering you

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Looking up plants

If you type a plant that isn't in the app's seed tin, the app can look it up using Apple Intelligence on supported devices. This runs on your device using Apple's on-device model; what you type isn't sent to us. Apple's own privacy policy covers Apple Intelligence.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules one on your device to tell you when your plant is ready. It's created and delivered on your device; no server is involved.

Purchases

The app is distributed by Apple through the App Store. If you pay for anything, Apple takes the payment and we never see your card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

How long data is kept

Everything stays on your device until you remove a plant from your garden (touch and hold it, then Remove from garden) or delete the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own device, deleting the app removes everything. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
