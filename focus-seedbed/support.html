Support

Focus Seedbed, by Danny Bartlett

Questions, problems or ideas for plants to add? Email danny@pcce.co.uk and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Your garden is saved on your device.

Does the timer keep going if I leave the app? Yes. It runs on the clock, so it keeps counting while you use other apps, and you'll get a notification when your plant is ready. The end-of-session summary tells you how many times you switched away.

Why didn't my plant go into my garden? Plants only join your garden when the timer finishes. If you choose End early, that plant isn't kept.

How do I find a plant that isn't in the seed tin? Type its name and tap Find seed. Looking up new plants needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence (iOS 26 or later), with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. Everywhere else, the 179 plants in the seed tin all work.

How do I remove a plant from my garden? Touch and hold it, then choose Remove from garden.

I'm not getting a notification when the timer ends. Go to Settings → Notifications → Focus Seedbed and turn on Allow Notifications.
