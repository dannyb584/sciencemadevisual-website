Privacy policy

Interchange · Last updated 10 November 2026

Who we are

Interchange ("the app") is made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). For any privacy question, email danny@pcce.co.uk.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The whole network is built into the app, so the stations you search for and the journeys you plan never leave your phone.
  • From version 1.1, the app's one internet request is for TfL's public line status (see below). It sends nothing about you.
  • The app doesn't use your location and doesn't ask for any permissions.

What the app keeps, and where

DataWhere it's keptWhy
Your last six journeys (the start and end stations)On your device, inside the appTo show them under "Recent" so you can plan them again with one tap
Emails you send usOur mailboxAnswering you

Your recent journeys aren't sent to us or to anyone else, and they aren't synced to iCloud.

Line status from TfL

From version 1.1, when your phone has a signal, the app asks Transport for London's open data service for the current status of each line: when you open the app, and at most once every five minutes (or when you pull down to refresh the status list). The request is the same for everyone. It carries no account, no identifier, no cookies, no location and nothing you searched for or planned; like any website, TfL's servers see the internet address the request comes from, under TfL's own privacy notice. The app keeps TfL's last answer on your device so it can show it, with its time, when you have no signal, and your routes are still planned on your phone.

Links in the app

The About screen links to this page and to our support page. They open in Safari, and the app sends nothing with them.

Purchases

The app is distributed by Apple through the App Store. Apple takes the payment and we never see your card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to the payment.

How long data is kept

Your recent journeys stay until you clear them: open About (the "i" button) and choose Forget recent journeys. Deleting the app removes them too.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). Because the app holds nothing about you outside your own device, you're in control of all of it, as described above. Email us for anything else.

Changes

We'll update this page if the app changes what it collects, and change the date at the top.
