Support

Interchange, by Danny Bartlett

Questions, problems or ideas? Email danny@pcce.co.uk and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days.

Common questions

Do I need a signal? No. The whole network is built into the app, so it plans journeys in a tunnel, on a plane or abroad with data roaming off. Only the live line status (from version 1.1) needs a signal.

How do I plan a journey? Tap From and type a station, then tap Where to? and type another. Or tap a station on the map and choose Start here or Go here. Tap a journey to see its steps.

What does "Northbound" or "Eastbound" mean? It's the direction on the platform signs, so you know which platform to head for. Where the signs don't use compass directions, as on the DLR and Overground, the app shows where the train is heading instead.

Is the platform number always right? It's the platform trains usually leave from. Trains are sometimes moved to another platform, so follow the station's own signs and boards.

Does it know about closures, strikes or delays? From version 1.1, partly. When you have a signal, Interchange shows TfL's line status as you open it, with TfL's reason for any problem, and puts a warning on any line in your journey that isn't running a good service. It still plans every route from the full network and doesn't steer around closures, so check the station boards or TfL before you travel. With no signal it shows the last status it fetched, with its time, or nothing if that's more than three hours old.

Which lines are included? The 11 Tube lines, the Elizabeth line, the DLR and the six Overground lines (Liberty, Lioness, Mildmay, Suffragette, Weaver and Windrush). Trams, the cable car, buses and river boats aren't included.

Can it plan step-free journeys? Not yet.

How up to date is the network? About (the "i" button) shows the date of the network data. New stations and timetable changes come with app updates.

How do I clear my recent journeys? About → Forget recent journeys.

Is it made by TfL? No. Interchange is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Transport for London. It's built from TfL Open Data.
