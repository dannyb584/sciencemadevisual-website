Interchange is an offline map and journey planner for London's Tube, Elizabeth line, DLR and Overground, made by Danny Bartlett ("we"). Your use of the app is also covered by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

2. Journeys and times

Journeys are planned on your phone from a copy of the network built into the app. Times are estimates from the timetable, with a few minutes added for each change, and platform numbers are the ones trains usually use. The app doesn't know about closures, engineering works, strikes, delays or step-free access, so check the station boards and TfL before you travel. Interchange is a planning aid, not a guarantee of any service.
